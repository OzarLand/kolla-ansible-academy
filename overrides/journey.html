How to use the journey

Open a page of the stage you are at, read it, then tick Mark this page as read at the bottom. The bar under the title of each page tells you where you are, and the buttons take you to the previous or next step. Your progress is kept in this browser only: nothing is sent anywhere.

Why this order

The first stages explain how the pages are written and what happens when a command runs. With that in mind, the procedures of the later stages make sense instead of being recipes to copy.

Lumo: practise safely

The study is a reading of the source code: nothing in it was run against a host. Try what you learn on a test platform, never first on production.

Three levels

Each page of this study shows its level under its title, so that you know what to expect:

  • Discover the conventions, the essentials and the concepts: start here.
  • Practise the operations, the risks and troubleshooting: once the concepts are clear.
  • Master the components in depth and the detailed evidence: to go further.
